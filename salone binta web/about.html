ABOUT TOURISM SALONE

Our Mission local discovery.

Salone Explorer is a student web design project concept created to make information about Sierra Leone's destinations, local services and trip planning easier to find in one place.

The platform responds to a common challenge: visitors may hear about beautiful beaches, wildlife and heritage sites, but struggle to find clear information or trusted local contacts.

Our goal is to make discovery simpler while giving local tourism providers a clearer way to be found. The prototype demonstrates how a well-organised website can support that goal.
